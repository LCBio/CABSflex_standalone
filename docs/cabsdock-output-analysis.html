CABS-dock Output & Analysis¶

This page describes the output models, plots, and additional analysis generated by CABS-dock.

1. Output Models¶

The resulting models are stored in the /output_pdb directory within the working directory. CABS-dock generates the following files:

  • model_*.pdb — Top-scored models (default: 10) in all-atom representation, numbered 1 to 10. These are the recommended final results.
  • cluster_*.pdb — Clusters of models in CA representation. cluster_7.pdb corresponds to model_7.pdb. These files can be used for visual assessment of clustering quality.
  • top_*.pdb — Top-scored models in CA representation (default: top1000.pdb) selected for clustering from the 10 trajectories.
  • replica_*.pdb — Complete set of trajectories in CA representation (default: 10 replicas × 1000 models). These are the raw output of the simulation.

2. Output Plots and Analysis¶

Plots are stored in the /plots and /contact_maps subdirectories.

2.1 RMSD Plot Analysis¶

Activated if --reference-pdb is provided.

  • RMSF (Root Mean Square Fluctuation) — Fluctuation of residues around the input position.

    • File: workdir/plots/RMSF_seq.svg (and workdir/RMSF.csv)

  • Energy vs. RMSD — Total energy and interaction energy vs. peptide RMSD.

    • File: workdir/plots/E_RMSD_<chain>_<energy>.svg (and .csv)

  • RMSD vs. MC step — History of RMSD changes for each replica.

    • File: workdir/plots/RMSD_frame_<chain>_replica_<num>.svg

2.2 Additional RMSD Data¶

Stored in /output_data: * all_rmsds_C.txt — RMSD for each simulation frame. * filtered_rmsds_C.txt — RMSD for frames filtered before scoring. * medoids_rmsds_C.txt — RMSD for top-scored models (cluster medoids). * lowest_rmsds_C.txt — Summary of lowest RMSD values.

3. Contact Maps and Histograms¶

Activated with the --contact-maps flag. Stored in /contact_maps.

  • Protein internal contact map — target_all.svg
  • Interface contact frequency maps — cluster_*_ch_*.svg, top_*_ch_*.svg, or replica_*_ch_*.svg.
  • Contact frequency histograms — all_contacts_histo_*.svg. Shows peptide residues, target residues in contact, and summary of all target residues.

4. Re-analysis of Finished Simulations¶

To perform additional analysis (e.g., calculate RMSD to a different reference or change contact cut-offs) without re-running the simulation, you must have run the original job with --save-cabs-files and --save-config.

Step 1: Original Run¶

CABSdock -i 2P1T:A -p HKILHRLLQD:CHHHHHHHHC --save-cabs-files --save-config

This saves config.ini and a .cbs file (e.g., 181116161924knWPtn.cbs).

Step 2: Re-analysis¶

Load the saved simulation and specify new analysis options:

CABSdock -c config.ini --load-cabs-files 181116161924knWPtn.cbs --reference-pdb 2P1T:AB --contact-maps

You can also change clustering/filtering parameters:

CABSdock -c config.ini --load-cabs-files SAVED_CBS_FILE --filtering-count 100 --clustering-medoids 3
